# 02 — Courses, lectures, and educational notes

Research pass: 2026-09-26. B/I/A and track labels are editorial. University material is globally useful; the collection emphasizes North American institutions where alternatives exist. Public course pages do not necessarily include grading, instructor support or student enrollment. Archived lectures remain useful but are not current market-rule references.

## Probability, mathematics, statistics

| Resource / source | Level; audience | Access and useful entry point |
|---|---|---|
| [Harvard Stat 110](https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/) | B–I; all | Free lectures, handouts and practice; start with counting, conditional probability and expectation. Companion book in category 01. |
| [MIT 18.05 Probability and Statistics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/) | B–I; all | Free notes, problems, exams and R resources; broader statistics pairing for Stat 110. |
| [MIT 18.650 Statistics for Applications](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-650-statistics-for-applications-fall-2016/) | I; QR | Free course materials; statistical inference after probability/calculus. |
| [MIT 18.06 Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/) | B–I; QR/QD | Free lectures and problem sets; matrix foundations for regression/optimization. |
| [3Blue1Brown — linear algebra collection](https://www.3blue1brown.com/?topic=linear-algebra) | B; all | Free visual intuition; supplement rather than replace exercises. |
| [Stanford EE364a Convex Optimization I](https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee364a/) | A; QR/FE | Public course materials; linear algebra and multivariable calculus first. |
| [StatQuest video index](https://statquest.org/video_index.html) | B–I; QR | Topic-indexed explanations for statistics/ML; pair with applied work. Source found through creator's indexed pages. |

## Additional foundations and applications

- [MIT 6.006 Introduction to Algorithms](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-spring-2020/): I; QD; public lectures/problem sets after basic programming.
- [MIT 6.262 Discrete Stochastic Processes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-262-discrete-stochastic-processes-spring-2011/): I–A; stochastic processes after probability; public archived materials.
- [Hamilton's graduate teaching materials](https://econweb.ucsd.edu/~jhamilto/teaching.html): A; time-series notes and software accompanying his book; archived teaching material.
- [EDHEC Investment Management with Python and Machine Learning](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/investment-management-python-machine-learning): I–A; portfolio applications; platform enrollment/access conditions apply.
- [UBC Mathematical Python](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~pwalls/math-python/): Canadian numerical-computing candidate; redirected university destination established but page extraction limited. Inspect lesson availability before a public recommendation.
- [Official NumPy learning resources](https://numpy.org/learn/) and [SciPy tutorials](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/tutorial/): I; practical numerical foundations; examples rather than a single graded course.
