# 10 — Practitioner writing, talks, podcasts and communities

Checked 2026-09-26. These are discovery sources, not blanket endorsements of every article or episode. The public hub should select specific items, with level and topic labels, rather than syndicating feeds automatically. Commercial firms and educators naturally present their own perspectives.

## What to publish first

Start with about 10 individually selected articles/talks across probability, market structure, research validation and engineering. For each, write 2–3 sentences: what it teaches, prerequisites, and why a student would choose it. Add a member's named note only after they actually read or watched it. Keep podcasts as supplemental discovery; they should not displace structured courses or hands-on work.

Man and Research Affiliates were considered, but the attempted landing pages could not be reliably fetched; retain them as source leads rather than reviewed recommendations. Broad finance-news feeds were deliberately omitted because maintaining them would dilute the learning purpose.
