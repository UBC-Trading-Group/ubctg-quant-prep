# 03 — Official interview resources and North American firm discovery

Research pass: 2026-09-26. This is a directory, not a list of guaranteed current vacancies, visa sponsorship, or firms endorsing UBCTG. Geography means US/Canadian opportunity discovery; a global careers page must be filtered by location. General bank and pension-fund portals include many non-quant roles. Identify the team and actual responsibilities before classifying a posting as QT, QR, QD or FE.

## Verification limitations

- The second pass added smaller trading firms, systematic managers, Canadian institutions and US banks above. CPP Investments remained bot-protected; attempted National Bank, Trans-Canada Capital and JPMorgan quant-program destinations were not reliably retrieved. Those are researched but unresolved leads, not verified application links.
- Check US/Canada locations against official office or job evidence for all global firms before publishing geographical badges. Current entries are discovery leads, not a validated office-location database.
- Some searches returned similarly named unrelated companies or lookalike domains. Do not import those results. Use the official sources above.
- For NA job advice, record employer-stated eligibility and work-authorization requirements per posting rather than assuming Canadian students can accept any US internship.
