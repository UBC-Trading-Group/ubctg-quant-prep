## Additional collections and practice routes

- [MAA Putnam](https://maa.org/putnam/): advanced mathematical problems with official past solutions/archive links. Optional enrichment; substantially different from ordinary quant interviews.
- [Waterloo CEMC past contests](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/resources/past-contests): Canadian mathematics/computing contest questions, solutions and results; select by level, not simply newest edition.
- [Metaculus](https://www.metaculus.com/): probabilistic forecasting/calibration practice; record predictions and evaluate outcomes. Not a bank of employer interview questions.
- [pandas introductory exercises/examples](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/getting_started/intro_tutorials/): data manipulation after SQLBolt; complements algorithm problems with actual data work.

For estimation, turn public Statistics Canada/Census series from category 06 into Fermi questions, showing assumptions before comparing to the published value. This is an original club activity proposal, not a claim that those agencies offer interview puzzles. AoPS was investigated but its archive fetch was blocked; no reviewed entry is claimed. Solution availability varies within each archive; the public site should label selected individual problems after inspection. Commercial question banks are in category 05.
