# Quant finance resource catalog

Research started: 2026-09-26 (America/Los_Angeles).

## Objective

Collect an exhaustive, category-by-category collection of resources for the UBCTG public learning hub. Use North American firms and opportunities where geography matters; retain excellent worldwide books, courses, software, and research. This is a research inventory, not a claim that UBCTG members have personally reviewed every resource or that every resource should be published.

Research collection completed: 2026-09-26. All ten categories received a collection pass; the initial coverage gaps received a second pass. The category files contain 333 external link references (including deliberate cross-listings, supporting links and explicitly marked candidates). This is a broad research inventory, not literally every resource on the internet. See AUDIT.md for access limitations and the distinction between collected sources and publication-ready recommendations.

## Files and coverage

1. `01-books.md`: books, textbooks, interview books, accessible introductions, technical references.
2. `02-courses.md`: courses, recorded lectures, lecture notes, exchange education.
3. `03-firms-and-interviews.md`: official interview resources, US/Canadian firm discovery and student careers.
4. `04-puzzles.md`: puzzles, mathematical problem sets, coding exercises.
5. `05-practice.md`: practice platforms, games, interactive learning.
6. `06-data.md`: datasets, APIs, filings, economic and market data.
7. `07-projects-and-code.md`: starter projects, notebooks, software, reproducible examples.
8. `08-competitions.md`: competitions, challenges, North American eligibility and event status.
9. `09-papers.md`: foundational papers, research collections, replication material.
10. `10-talks-and-writing.md`: practitioner articles, technical talks, podcasts, communities.
11. `AUDIT.md`: coverage, source limitations, outstanding work, publication recommendations.

## Reading the entries

- B = beginner; I = intermediate; A = advanced. These are editorial placement judgments, not provider certifications.
- QT = trading; QR = research; QD = development; FE = derivatives/pricing/risk.
- Free means reading/material is publicly available, not that certificates, compute, data redistribution, or all product features are free.
- Paid/library means a commercial book or subscription; check your university library. No prices are frozen into this inventory.
- A link is evidence for identity and described subject matter; it does not establish pedagogical quality or a guaranteed career outcome.
- Source checked means the public page or an indexed source was inspected during this research. It does not mean every lesson was completed or software executed.
- Exact event dates and eligibility require organizer evidence; an old competition page does not prove that registration is open now.
- Named chapter suggestions and ordering are editorial recommendations. Do not invent completion times or member endorsements.

## Publication approach

Keep the exhaustive inventory behind a selective public guide. Expose a few recommended starting resources per topic, with an expandable alternatives list. Use topic pages (probability, market making, data for projects) as well as format filters. Books and their companion courses should cross-link instead of appearing as unrelated recommendations.

Do not publish automatically from this directory. The user requested collection and research, not deployment.
