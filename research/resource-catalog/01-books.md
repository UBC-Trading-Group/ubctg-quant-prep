# 01 — Books and textbooks

Research pass: 2026-09-26. Audience and suggested use are editorial judgments. Source links verify identity/subject, not personal testing. Books are globally useful; North American availability can usually be checked through university libraries. No regional edition is mandatory unless exercises or pagination need to match a course.

## Probability, statistics, and mathematical foundations

| Resource / author | Level; audience | Access | Suggested use / source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction to Probability — Joseph Blitzstein & Jessica Hwang | B–I; all | Free online; paid print | Core probability text; pair exercises with Stat 110. [Harvard book link and description](https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/). |
| Think Stats, 3rd ed. — Allen Downey | B; QR/QD | Free online; paid print | Computational statistics for someone who already knows basic Python. [Author book](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkStats/). |
| Think Bayes, 2nd ed. — Allen Downey | B–I; QT/QR | Free online; paid print | Bayesian updating through runnable notebooks. [Author book](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkBayes2/). |
| All of Statistics — Larry Wasserman | I–A; QR | Paid/library; companion material public | Compact mathematical statistics reference after calculus and probability. [Author page](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~larry/all-of-statistics/). |
| Convex Optimization — Stephen Boyd & Lieven Vandenberghe | A; QR/FE | Free author-hosted book; paid print | Optimization foundations for portfolio and estimation work; linear algebra first. [Author book](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/). |
| The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to Digital Signal Processing — Steven W. Smith | I; QR/QD | Free online; paid print | Supplemental background on filtering and signals; not a finance-specific starting text. [Author site](https://www.dspguide.com/). |

## Statistical learning, time series, and econometrics

| Resource / author | Level; audience | Access | Suggested use / source |
|---|---|---|---|
| An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in R — James, Witten, Hastie & Tibshirani | I; QR | Free PDF; paid print | Statistical learning with chapter labs; choose this or the Python edition initially. [Official site](https://www.statlearning.com/). |
| An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in Python — James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani & Taylor | I; QR/QD | Free PDF; paid print | Practical first ML text after basic statistics and Python. [Official site](https://www.statlearning.com/). |

| Probabilistic Machine Learning book series — Kevin Murphy | I–A; QR | Author-hosted material; print paid | Broad probabilistic ML reference; distinguish Introduction from Advanced Topics. [Author series](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/). |

| Machine Learning for Factor Investing — Guillaume Coqueret & Tony Guida | I–A; QR | Free online; paid print | Connects ML to factor research and portfolio evaluation. [Author book](https://www.mlfactor.com/). |
| Advances in Financial Machine Learning — Marcos López de Prado | A; QR | Paid/library | Research design, labels, validation and financial ML; not a first ML course. [Authorized book listing and contents](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/advances-in-financial/9781119482086/). |
