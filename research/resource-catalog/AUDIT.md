## Evidence and limits

- Primary sources were preferred: authors/publishers, university course sites, employer/organizer pages and official project documentation. Search-indexed official evidence was used when direct extraction failed; those limitations are called out where material.
- Descriptions establish source identity and broad subject matter. No books/courses were completed for this task, no games played, no paid subscriptions purchased, and no software or trading strategy executed.
- Level, track, suggested use and starting exercises are editorial judgments. No member endorsement, career outcome or strategy profitability is asserted.
- Exact prices and fragile current-vacancy links were generally avoided. Firm portals need role/location filtering. Office presence does not imply sponsorship or eligibility for a particular student.
- Competition dates are snapshots. CME's 2026 edition requires a faculty adviser even though evergreen wording differed. US/Canada labels must not conceal exclusions: Quebec restrictions and US-based university rules are recorded where found.
- Data-source pages do not establish all history, frequency, point-in-time coverage or redistribution rights. These require dataset-specific selection before implementation.
- Some pages were script-rendered, bot-blocked, redirected or unavailable. This is not evidence the underlying organization/resource is bad or permanently offline.

## Investigated but not fully verified

Retain these as research notes rather than prominent public recommendations:

- Books: primary destinations for Heard on the Street and Joshi interview book; some editions require library/publisher confirmation. Zhou has bibliographic evidence. More specialist credit/rates texts can follow actual club coursework.
- Courses: Berkeley CS61B and Montréal Exchange destination attempts failed; UBC Mathematical Python content extraction was limited. Georgia Tech overview does not prove unrestricted assignment/video access.
- Employers: CPP Investments, National Bank, Trans-Canada Capital and JPMorgan quantitative-program routes could not all be confirmed through accessible pages. Other global portals remain discovery sources rather than a fully verified office/job inventory.
- Puzzles/practice: AoPS archive and C1games direct access were unavailable; commercial question banks were not tested behind accounts. No official Optiver practice tool is claimed without verification.
- Data: SEDAR+ bot protection, Global-q/Treasury reader failures and limited Finnhub/Stooq/LOBSTER extraction. Open Source Asset Pricing has a repository alternative. CRSP research products must be distinguished from a redirected index homepage.
- Code: QSTrader site attempt failed; selected software still needs installation/version checks when adopted.
- Competitions: Bloomberg organizer portal could not be reliably inspected. Some campus competitions have only completed-edition evidence; no future date invented.
- Papers/writing: author/publisher download for Almgren–Chriss not established; an unrelated mirror was not promoted. SSRN, Man and Research Affiliates access was limited.

These are disclosed evidence boundaries of the collection, not a hidden claim that everything has been personally vetted. They should stay out of the featured public subset until checked.

## Recommended public shape

Organize navigation around visitor tasks, with format filters underneath:

1. **Start learning**: probability, programming, statistics and market mechanics; a few choices per topic.
2. **Prepare for interviews**: official role guidance, selected exercises, games and optional books.
3. **Build a project**: one dataset, one worked example, prerequisites and a bounded target.
4. **Find opportunities**: NA competitions and official firm student pages, with checked dates.

Keep papers and practitioner talks as a deeper layer. Add write-ups naturally when members finish a project, compete, or lead a reading session. No daily news feed is needed.

For each featured item record title, original source, topic, level, prerequisite, access, role relevance, short reason to choose it, last-checked date, and whether a member actually used it. A useful public topic page might pair one probability course, one puzzle collection and one UBCTG game. The value comes from the choices and connections, not the size of the directory.

## Validation performed

All ten category files are present. Markdown external link references were counted locally; counts include support/candidate links and duplicates. Sources were inspected during research by web reader or indexed primary result. This was not a browser-based click-through test of every destination or a full automated HTTP link audit. No website files were deployed, and no external messages/applications were sent.
