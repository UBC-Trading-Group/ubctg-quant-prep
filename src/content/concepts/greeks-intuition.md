Greeks describe how an option price responds to a small change in an input.

| Greek | Rough meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Delta | Sensitivity to the underlying price |
| Gamma | Sensitivity of delta to the underlying price |
| Theta | Sensitivity to time passing |
| Vega | Sensitivity to implied volatility |
| Rho | Sensitivity to interest rates |

## Intuition

Delta is the first lens interviewers often expect. Gamma explains why delta itself changes. Theta and vega are especially important because options are exposed to time and volatility, not just direction.

## Common Mistake

Greeks are local approximations. Large price moves can make the original sensitivities stale.
