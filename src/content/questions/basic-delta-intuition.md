## Problem

A call option has delta $0.6$. Roughly what happens to the option price if the stock rises by 1?

## Hint 1

Delta is the local price sensitivity to the underlying.

## Hint 2

Ignore gamma for a very small move.

## Solution

The option price should rise by about $0.6$. This is a local approximation; for larger stock moves, gamma changes the delta.
