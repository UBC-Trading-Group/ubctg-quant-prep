## Problem

Estimate the number of piano tuners in a city of 2 million people. Give a rough interval and explain your assumptions.

## Hint 1

Break the estimate into households, piano ownership, and tuners per piano.

## Hint 2

A good interview answer is transparent about uncertainty.

## Solution

One path:

- About 800,000 households.
- Suppose 4 percent own pianos: 32,000 pianos.
- Suppose each piano needs one tuning per year.
- Suppose a tuner does about 800 tunings per year.

That gives:

$$
32000 / 800 = 40
$$

A reasonable interval might be 20 to 80 tuners, depending on ownership and utilization assumptions.
