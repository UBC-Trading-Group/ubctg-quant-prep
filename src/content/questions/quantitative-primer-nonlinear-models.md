## Problem

What are some non-linear models?

## Hint 1

Think beyond ordinary linear regression.

## Hint 2

Include both statistical and machine-learning examples.

## Solution

Examples include:

- logistic regression, because the response probability is a nonlinear function of $X\beta$,
- probit regression,
- generalized additive models,
- decision trees and random forests,
- gradient-boosted trees,
- neural networks,
- nonlinear least-squares models,
- regime-switching models,
- stochastic volatility models.

In an interview, give examples you can explain. It is better to name fewer models and discuss their assumptions, estimation, and failure modes than to list many you cannot defend.
